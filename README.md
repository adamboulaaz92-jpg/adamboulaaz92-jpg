### Who am I?
Hey there! I am Adam! Im a 15 yo Moroccan dev who loves Networking and Network Security!!! I have a huge interest in all things tech. I don't have just one goal, rather I want to build custom networking tools from scratch and learn as much as I can about how the internet works—not just to build cool stuff, but also for the thrill and fun!

### Here are my interests outside of strict programming:
* **Cybersecurity:** Exploring vulnerabilities, learning how systems communicate, and testing networks!
* **Tech Hardware:** Tinkering with machines (like booting new OSes on my HP EliteDesk!) and seeing how different hardware handles networking.
* **Open Source:** I love contributing to and maintaining open-source projects!

### When did I start programming?
I started diving into scripts and networking a while ago. Learning how packets actually work under the hood and figuring out how upper layers interact with lower layers like Ethernet and ARP made jumping into complex Python projects surprisingly fun.

### Low level langs?
Someday. Since I deal with raw packets and network sockets, I think learning C or C++ would be incredibly useful down the line!

### Planning to get into Red Teaming / Pentesting?
I think it'd be really fun to learn! What I love about network security is that you're literally discovering how systems communicate, brainstorming ways to map them out, and building tools to see exactly what's happening under the surface!

Linux all the way! I spend a lot of my time working in security-focused distros. I use Parrot OS, and let me tell you, AMAZING. I even flash it onto USBs to boot on my machines. It just gives you so much control over your environment, especially for packet manipulation!

### What do you think are your coolest projects?
I am the main maintainer for the **Light-Projects** organization!!!
* **Light-Scan** - A custom CLI port scanner! It's designed for speed, and I am currently working on adding comprehensive service probes (like the ones Nmap uses) for banner grabbing!
* **LightPacket** - A multi-layer network packet manipulation library! It parses protocols and tracks how upper layers interact with foundational parameters.
